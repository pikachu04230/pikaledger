PiKa帳本 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-25

總覽

PiKa帳本（PiKaLedger，以下稱「本 App」）是 Android 個人記帳 App。本 App 沒有帳號系統，也沒有開發者經營的伺服器；開發者不會收到、看到或保存你的帳本資料。本 App 不含廣告、分析或當機回報 SDK。

手機上的資料

帳本、旅程、規則與設定只存在你的手機上，受 Android 的檔案加密保護，且不參與 Android 系統備份。解除安裝或清除本 App 資料會刪除它們。你匯出的備份檔與自動備份到手機資料夾的檔案是未加密的 JSON，存放位置由你選擇，也由你管理。

匯率更新

開啟「自動更新匯率」時，本 App 會向 Frankfurter（api.frankfurter.dev）下載公開匯率。請求不含你的帳本資料、幣別偏好或任何識別碼；對方會看到你的 IP 位址，這是網路連線固有的。關閉此設定後，本 App 不再下載匯率。

Google 雲端硬碟備份（選用）

  • 存取範圍：只有在你主動連結時，本 App 才透過 Google Play 服務請求 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata 權限。這個權限只能存取本 App 在你雲端硬碟中建立的隱藏應用程式資料夾，無法讀取或修改你雲端硬碟中的其他檔案。本 App 不請求你的個人資料、聯絡人或其他 Google 資料。
  • 使用方式：本 App 把自動備份檔上傳到該隱藏資料夾，保留最近 10 份，刪除更舊的本 App 備份，並可讓你從中還原。檔案中附帶交易筆數、檔案大小與校驗碼，用來確認上傳完整。
  • 儲存：備份檔存在你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟，佔用你的儲存空間。本 App 在手機上只記錄連結的帳號 email、時間與最近一次備份的結果；不保存存取權杖（由 Google Play 服務管理）。上傳到雲端硬碟的備份檔同樣未加密，由你的 Google 帳號保護。
  • 分享：你的 Google 使用者資料只在你的手機與 Google 之間傳輸，不會傳給開發者或任何第三方，也不用於廣告或其他用途。
  • 移除：你可以在本 App 中中斷連結，或到 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤銷本 App 的存取權。要刪除雲端上的備份，可在 Google 雲端硬碟網頁版的「設定 → 管理應用程式」中，對本 App 選擇「刪除隱藏的應用程式資料」。

本 App 對從 Google API 取得的資訊之使用與傳輸，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 規定。

診斷資訊

App 只在手機上保留最近一次當機的紀錄，內容是時間與程式位置，不含帳目或你輸入的內容。診斷資訊檔只有在你點「傳送診斷資訊」或長按桌面圖示時才會建立，由你選擇的 App 送出，內容不含帳目。

兒童

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不刻意收集兒童的資料。

變更與聯絡

本政策變更時會更新此頁與生效日期。如有疑問，請來信：pikaledger.app@gmail.com。

PiKaLedger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Overview

PiKaLedger ("the app") is a personal finance app for Android. It has no account system and no developer-operated server. The developer never receives, sees, or stores your ledger data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

Data on your phone

Your ledger, trips, rules, and settings are stored only on your phone, protected by Android file encryption, and excluded from Android system backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. Backup files you export, and automatic backups written to a phone folder, are unencrypted JSON stored where you choose and managed by you.

Exchange-rate updates

When "Update exchange rates automatically" is on, the app downloads public rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). Requests contain no ledger data, currency preferences, or identifiers. The server sees your IP address, as with any network connection. With the setting off, the app does not download rates.

Google Drive backup (optional)

  • Access: Only when you choose to link Google Drive does the app request, through Google Play services, the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This scope reaches only the app's own hidden application-data folder in your Drive; it cannot read or change any other file in your Drive. The app requests no profile, contacts, or other Google data.
  • Use: The app uploads its automatic backup files to that hidden folder, keeps the 10 most recent, deletes older backups made by the app, and lets you restore from them. Each file carries its transaction count, size, and checksum so the app can confirm the upload is complete.
  • Storage: Backup files are stored in your own Google Drive and count against your storage. On your phone, the app records only the linked account email, times, and the result of the latest backup. It does not store access tokens; Google Play services manages them. Backup files uploaded to Google Drive are not encrypted either; they are protected by your Google account.
  • Sharing: Your Google user data travels only between your phone and Google. It is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is not used for advertising or any other purpose.
  • Removal: You can disconnect Google Drive in the app, or revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete backups stored in Drive, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose "Delete hidden app data" for the app.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Diagnostic information

The app keeps only a record of its most recent crash on your phone: the time and where in the code it happened, with no ledger entries or anything you typed. A diagnostic file is created only when you tap “傳送診斷資訊” (Send diagnostic info) in Settings or long-press the app icon; it is sent by the app you choose and contains no ledger entries.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. Questions: pikaledger.app@gmail.com.
